## Host and physical validation are distinct

The canonical host validation remains **45/45**. Its deterministic one-frame
callback produced 652 meshes, 30,603 vertices, 16,939 triangles, and checksum
`34FFAD42`. The physical runtime produced 654 meshes and checksum `0C3D50E4`
on its first presented world frame while using the physical timing/camera/
visibility path. These are separate validation sets and traversal conditions;
their check totals and frame fingerprints are not forced to match. Both agree
on the semantic world fingerprints above and both report no unsupported or
rejected geometry.

## Evidence limitation

Visual fidelity is **NOT FULLY ASSESSED FROM LOG EVIDENCE**. The runtime log
proves original world initialization, camera application, continuous WW3D
traversal, frame presentation, START processing, zero render errors, and clean
teardown. It does not by itself prove exact texture, material, blending, or
lighting appearance, and this baseline makes no pixel-perfect claim.

No retail game data is packaged in or copied into the baseline. The immutable
archive is `../../baselines/A3.0/`; its patch and baseline-wide checksum
manifests verify successfully.
