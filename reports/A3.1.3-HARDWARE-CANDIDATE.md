# A3.1.3 hardware-equivalent stabilization candidate

Status: **superseded by returned physical evidence; frozen as the first
physically accepted original interactive-lifecycle baseline.** It is not a
visible-gameplay baseline: the original interactive lifecycle ran for 1,354
frames and shut down cleanly, but its interactive render metrics were zero
meshes, vertices, and triangles. The raw Vita log and observation are immutable
under `baselines/A3.1.3/`; its stale `a31.1-runtime.log` filename/header are
preserved as evidence.

## Why A3.1.2 passed host but crashed on Vita

The prior host harness called `HUDClass::Enable(false)` after M00 post-load, while the Vita runtime did not. Retail post-load restored `_HUDEnabled`; `CombatManager::Think()` then entered `HUDClass::Think()` with `InfoRenderer == NULL`, because temporary `CombatManager::Init(false)` had intentionally not constructed Font3D-dependent HUD resources.

## Repair

There is now one shared A3.1 interactive policy and frame sequence for host and Vita. The policy makes Font3D/HUD capability explicit and both use `CombatManager::Init(A31_Interactive_Render_HUD_Available())`. The hidden host-only correction was removed.

`_HUDEnabled` remains serialized/user state. A separate central resource-availability state gates `HUDClass::Think`, `Render`, `Reset`, and displayability. It also prevents Sniper HUD and other render-dependent owners from being reached in temporary no-HUD mode. HUD shutdown handles both full and partial initialization. Teardown resets `cGod` before network cleanup, which the two-cycle test proved necessary.

The full execution matrix is delivered as `A3.1.3-EXECUTION_EQUIVALENCE.md` and describes the small permitted host/Vita adapter differences.
