# Contributor conduct

This project welcomes precise, good-faith work on a difficult preservation and portability problem.

- Discuss evidence, code, and technical decisions respectfully.
- Do not misrepresent host, emulator, or hardware results.
- Do not share retail data, credentials, personal media, or private device details.
- Keep criticism specific to the work; harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not acceptable.
- Respect the project's source-ownership, licensing, and evidence boundaries.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
