# A3.5-dev204: LiveArea identity

**Experimental native ARM development build.** Dev204 supersedes Dev203's
first artwork layout. It adds an original, project-authored R/V insignia,
application icon, launch image, and LiveArea background and gate. The candidate
number is rendered into the artwork. No EA or Westwood logo was copied.

## Verified

- 177 focused tests and fast ARM ELF/SELF/VPK identity checks passed.
- The retail-free VPK contains `eboot.bin`, `param.sfo`, and five
  project-authored LiveArea files.
- Vita3K installation and readback matched the VPK. The attached
  [LiveArea screenshot](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/RenegadeVita/releases/download/A3.5-dev204/dev204-livearea.png)
  shows the icon, background, gate, and readable `A3.5-dev204` label.

**Not verified:** Dev204 gameplay, a canonical build, physical PS Vita/PSTV
appearance, full campaign, full Practice or multiplayer, and sustained frame
rate. The executable carries Dev202's runtime changes, but this Dev204 check
was limited to title presentation.

## Install and provenance

Supply your own complete, patched retail data. **No retail data, saves,
credentials, or server packages are included.** Follow the
[installation guide](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/RenegadeVita/blob/main/docs/INSTALLING.md).
